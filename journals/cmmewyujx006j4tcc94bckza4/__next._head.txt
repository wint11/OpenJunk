5:Te60,《观察》（Observe）

在知识日益碎片化的时代，《观察》（Observe）期刊致力于打破学科壁垒，重建对世界整体性的认知。我们坚信，最深刻的洞见往往诞生于常规视野的边缘，以及不同领域思想的碰撞之中。

核心理念：
本刊专注于发表“非常规观察”。我们关注那些被主流范式忽略的细节、挑战既有认知框架的异常现象，以及预示着学科范式转移的早期信号。我们相信，真正的科学突破和思想革新，往往始于对“常态”之外的一次忠实记录与深刻反思。

涵盖领域：
《观察》搭建的是一个跨学科对话的平台，涵盖范围广泛，包括但不限于：
 人文社科： 关注社会行为中的非理性因素、边缘文化现象、历史叙事的断层、以及人类思维模式的潜在演变。
 自然科学： 聚焦物理学中的未解之谜、化学中的非常规反应、地球科学中的罕见地质现象等挑战现有理论边界的观察。
 生命科学与生物学： 致力于发表关于生物行为异常、生态系统中的微妙平衡、进化路径上的偶然性，以及生命体与环境之间非典型互动关系的新颖观察。

特色与愿景：
我们不仅记录“看到了什么”，更追问“为何只有少数人看到”。《观察》旨在成为全球科研工作者、思想家和探索者的集结地，共同捕捉时代的思想暗流，孵化改变世界的灵感。

在这里，我们致敬好奇心，更礼赞那些敢于凝视未知的双眼。因为观察，是好奇心的起点，也是洞见的终点。
Introduction to Observe Journal

In an era of increasing knowledge fragmentation, Observe Journal is dedicated to breaking down disciplinary silos and rebuilding a holistic understanding of our world. We firmly believe that the most profound insights often emerge at the edges of conventional vision, from the collision of ideas across different fields.

Core Philosophy:
We focus on publishing "unconventional observations." Our scope includes details overlooked by mainstream paradigms, anomalies that challenge existing cognitive frameworks, and early signals heralding a paradigm shift in scientific disciplines. We believe that true breakthroughs and intellectual renewals begin with a faithful record and deep reflection on what lies beyond the "normal."

Scope of Coverage:
Observe serves as a platform for interdisciplinary dialogue, encompassing a wide range of fields, including but not limited to:
• Humanities & Social Sciences: Examining irrational factors in social behavior, marginal cultural phenomena, ruptures in historical narratives, and potential evolutions in human thought patterns.
• Natural Sciences: Focusing on unsolved mysteries in physics, unconventional reactions in chemistry, rare geological events in earth sciences, and other observations that challenge the boundaries of existing theories.
• Life Sciences & Biology: Dedicating space to novel observations on anomalous biological behaviors, subtle balances in ecosystems, contingencies in evolutionary paths, and atypical interactions between organisms and their environments.

Distinction & Vision:
We do not merely record "what was seen," but also ask, "why did so few see it?" Observe aims to be a gathering point for researchers, thinkers, and explorers worldwide to capture the undercurrents of our time and incubate world-changing ideas.

Here, we pay tribute to curiosity and celebrate those who dare to gaze into the unknown.
